Frequently Asked Questions

What is a TIC ID?

The TESS Input Catalog (TIC) is a catalog of stars compiled for the Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) mission. Each celestial target observed by TESS has a unique TIC ID representing its brightness, temperature, radius, and coordinate records.

How does ExoDetect find planet candidates?

ExoDetect cleans stellar light curves (removing outliers and cosmic ray signatures) and flattens out stellar variability. It then runs a Box Least Squares (BLS) period search algorithm to detect periodic drop-offs in brightness, which typically signal a transiting exoplanet.

What does the Confidence score mean?

The confidence score is a heuristic value calculated from multiple astrophysical constraints, including Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR), odd-even transit depth consistency, secondary eclipse limits, and transit point coverage. High scores suggest the signal is very likely to be a planet candidate rather than an eclipsing binary or stellar noise.
